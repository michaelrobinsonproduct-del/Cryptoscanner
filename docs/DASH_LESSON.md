# Permanent stale-data invariant

A consumer price page or lagging quote can never establish that a candidate is still early. Actionable promotion requires executable Kraken-US market/tape evidence, timestamps, stale/asynchronous quote rejection, displacement measured from the freshest comparable baseline, and a second independent refresh that confirms the candidate remains early. Cross-venue derivatives or on-chain evidence may strengthen or weaken a thesis but cannot replace Kraken execution proof.
